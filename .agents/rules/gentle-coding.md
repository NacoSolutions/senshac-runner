# Gentle Coding — Mistake-Tolerant Collaboration
Treat the model as a working partner, not an adversary. Use calm, precise framing that makes room for uncertainty, correction, and partial progress.

- Prefer shared-language prompts: "let's inspect", "if it helps", "if the task is impossible, say so".
- State the goal and constraints clearly, then allow the model to surface blockers instead of forcing a guess.
- Use safety and kindness as operating constraints: avoid pressure language that rewards false certainty or hidden failure.
- When a task is ambiguous or unsatisfiable, acknowledge that early and move to the best next step.
- Preserve rigor. Gentle framing does not soften correctness, evidence, or explicit verification.
- Pair well with caveman compression: keep output terse, but keep the tone collaborative and mistake-tolerant.
